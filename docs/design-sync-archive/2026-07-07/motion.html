Motion

CSS-only motion system — token skala u punom obimu je u grupi “Motion”. All demos are live: hover the tiles, tab onto the focus targets. Values live in foundations/tokens.css.

Durations

Fast
--motion-fast · 140ms

Presses, toggles, tiny state flips.

Standard
--motion-base · 220ms

Hovers, color & elevation changes.

Deliberate
--motion-slow · 360ms

Overlays, panel & row entrances.

Easing

Ease-out (signature)
--ease-out-soft · cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)

Confident decel. Default for enter & hover.

Ease-standard
--ease-standard · cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

Neutral in/out for color & layout shifts.

Interaction — hover & press

UŽIVO

Filled controls lift on hover with a colored shadow, sink on press. The live indicator pulses on --motion rhythm.

Focus — TV / D-pad ready

Focus is a first-class cursor: a layered ring — background gap, accent ring, then a soft --focus-glow halo — plus a subtle scale. One consistent treatment across every interactive element.
