Construction

One equilateral triangle.
Two cuts. Exact angles.

A vertical cut at 30% of the width is the stem. From its edge, two cuts at exactly 45° are the arm and the leg. Every gap is the same 12 units, every outer corner the same radius — so the mark stays calm and stable at 16 px and on a 75-inch TV.
